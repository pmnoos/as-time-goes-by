Useful Contacts for Older Australians

As we get older, it can sometimes be difficult to know where to turn for advice, support or information.

Following a recent Seniors Morning Tea, I collected details of several organisations that may be helpful to older Australians, people with disability and their families. I've brought these contacts together on one page for easy reference.

Rights and Advocacy

ADA Australia

ADA Australia provides advocacy and support for older people and people with disability, including assistance with understanding rights and navigating services.

Visit ADA Australia

Government Services and Concessions

Queensland Government

Find information about Queensland Government services, concessions, transport, housing and support available to residents.

Visit Queensland Government

Seniors' Information and Benefits

National Seniors Australia

National Seniors Australia provides information and advocacy on issues affecting older Australians. Membership also includes access to member benefits and discounts, including its EAT | PLAY | SAVE app. Check the organisation's website for current membership details.

Visit National Seniors Australia

Local Representation

Wendy Bourne — Member for Ipswich West

Contact your local representative for information about local issues, community concerns and assistance with matters that may require government attention.

Visit Wendy Bourne's website

Help at Home and Aged Care

My Aged Care

My Aged Care is the Australian Government's starting point for information about aged care services, eligibility and support options for older people.

Phone: 1800 200 422

Visit My Aged Care

A note from Peter: I've gathered these contacts as a starting point for readers who may find them useful. Organisations, services, contact details and eligibility requirements can change, so please check the relevant website or contact the organisation directly before making decisions.
